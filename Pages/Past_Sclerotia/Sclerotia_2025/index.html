The 2025 Sclerotia Project: a hopeful beginning

In 2025, the Sclerotia Project raised $15,000 from a growing community of supporters who believed in a long-term future for mycological science.

That effort mattered not only because of the amount raised, but because it brought more donors into the story. A wider circle of people investing in optimism can create momentum that lasts far beyond one campaign.

We are using this page to show what that early support could become over time, and to celebrate the donors who helped plant the first seeds.

What happened in 2025

  • • The project raised $15,000 from supporters who wanted to create a durable future for fungal science.
  • • The campaign emphasized community participation, small acts of generosity, and shared optimism.
  • • The 2025 effort helped establish the idea that long-term research can be supported by many donors, not just a few large gifts.

Why donors matter

The goal is not simply to raise more money. The goal is to grow a community of donors who believe the future of fungal science is worth building together.
