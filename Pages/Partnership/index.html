Partnership with the Mycological Society of America (MSA)

The Sclerotia Project is honored to be collaborating with the Mycological Society of America (MSA) to ensure the long-term stewardship of its financial legacy.

Each year's donations will be pooled with MSA’s larger endowment for security and growth, while still being tracked independently through dedicated accounts. These funds will be held in trust by MSA’s financial stewards, and allowed to grow for 100 years before dispersal.

When the time comes, the MSA Council and the MSA Student and Postdoc Committee will work together to determine how the funds are distributed. While the specifics will evolve with the needs of the future. However, our goal is: to support early-career mycologists, fund many over the few, and remain open to emerging global challenges in science and society.

Importantly, this partnership allows the Sclerotia Project to operate with transparency and legitimacy, linking grassroots fundraising to a professional institution with deep roots in mycological research and education. MSA has a proven track record of financial stewardship, and their experience managing endowments gives us confidence that your contributions will endure.
